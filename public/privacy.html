Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026.

Basira Provisions is a demonstration shop built for the HNG Internship. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google through Supabase Auth, and we store them only to identify your account and show your orders. When you place an order we store the items, total, the delivery address and phone number you enter, and we send one confirmation email to the address you give. We do not sell or share this data with anyone, we do not use advertising cookies, and we do not process payments online. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time by emailing the support address below, and we will remove your account and orders.

Data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication) and emails are sent through Mailgun. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages.
